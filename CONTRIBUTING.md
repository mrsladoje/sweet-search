# Contributing to sweet-search

Thanks for helping. Bug reports, benchmark results, and pull requests are all
welcome.

## Contributor License Agreement

Before we can merge your first pull request, you sign our
[Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) (CLA). A bot asks you on the pull
request. It takes one click, and it covers all your future pull requests.

Why we ask, in plain words:

- **You keep the copyright** in your code. The CLA is a license, not a
  transfer.
- **It lets us license sweet-search under other terms later**, for example a
  paid license for large companies. We say this up front so that nobody is
  surprised.
- **Released versions stay open source.** Every version released under
  Apache-2.0 stays available under Apache-2.0, for everyone, forever.

If you do not want to sign, open an issue instead. We can still use your bug
reports and ideas.
