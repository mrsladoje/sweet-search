\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

Measured at a fixed fraction of task quality instead of a recall target, the choice of candidate
generator matters most when the rest of the pipeline is held fixed: under one int8 screen and one
float16 rescoring, a binary scan or binary HNSW walk gave the same retained quality as float graphs
and quantised graphs at a fraction of their time, and partition indexes at their smallest effort lost
quality that the rescoring could not recover. As shipped, the binary cascade was faster than the
tested rivals at 0.5M--1M vectors on all five CPUs in most cells, and slower than an exact binary scan
in C++ below about 20k vectors. Its costs are memory, a slow single-threaded build and sensitivity
of a memory-mapped index to other I/O. Corpus size changed the answer more than the CPU did. These
results hold for the tested sets, models and CPUs, within the declared quality margin; the released
records let others check them and add systems.
